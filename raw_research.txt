Institutional Research Report: Jaypee University of Information Technology, WaknaghatCollege Overview and Statutory ApprovalsJaypee University of Information Technology (JUIT) was established by the Himachal Pradesh State Legislative Assembly under Act No. 14 of 2002 via an Extraordinary Gazette Notification dated May 23, 2002 [https://www.juit.ac.in/attachments/JUIT_Act.pdf], [https://www.juit.ac.in/attachments/SSRAUG2023.pdf].The university was created as a public-private partnership between the State Government of Himachal Pradesh and Jaiprakash Sewa Sansthan (JSS), a registered not-for-profit charitable trust; the State Government leased the project land to the trust [https://www.juit.ac.in/attachments/JUIT_Act.pdf], [https://www.juit.ac.in/attachments/SSRAUG2023.pdf], [https://www.juit.ac.in/department/pd/BBA.pdf].Academic operations commenced in July 2002 with undergraduate degree programs in Electronics & Communication Engineering, Computer Science & Engineering, Information Technology, and Bioinformatics; Civil Engineering was added in July 2003 [https://www.juit.ac.in/attachments/FinalJUITSSRAPRIL2017.pdf].JUIT is the first State Private University established in Himachal Pradesh [https://www.juit.ac.in/department/pd/BBA.pdf].The campus occupies 25 acres at Waknaghat, Kandaghat Tehsil, Solan District, Himachal Pradesh [https://www.juit.ac.in/department/pd/BBA.pdf], [https://www.juit.ac.in/attachments/JUIT_Act.pdf].Statutory approval was granted by the University Grants Commission (UGC) under Section 2(f) of the UGC Act, 1956, vide letter no. F. 9-10/2002(CPP-I) dated December 09, 2002 [https://www.juit.ac.in/attachments/FinalJUITSSRAPRIL2017.pdf], [https://www.juit.ac.in/attachments/SSRAUG2023.pdf].The university functions under the regulatory oversight of the Himachal Pradesh Private Educational Institutions Regulatory Commission (HPPERC) [https://www.juit.ac.in/importantnotification].AICTE mandatory disclosures are published on the institutional portal, and admission refund schedules comply with AICTE/UGC guidelines [https://www.juit.ac.in/NAAC2022/NAACFILES/5/5_1/5.1.4%20Redressal%20of%20student%20grievances/5.1.4%20Link%20for%20Additional%20Info%20%28all%20Documents%20Final%29.pdf], [https://www.juit.ac.in/admissions/admission-withdrawal-refund].
